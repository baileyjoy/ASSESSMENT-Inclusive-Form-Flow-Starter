Grow With Us!

Help make our neighborhood a little greener. Whether you are new to gardening or have years of experience, there is a place for you!

F1 · Purpose and Expectations

This form is for community members interested in volunteering at a local community garden. Volunteers may help with planting, watering, weeding, harvesting, and garden maintenance.

Completing this form takes approximately 3–5 minutes. In a real program, a garden coordinator would review the request and contact the volunteer about opportunities.

Required fields: Questions marked (required) must be completed. All other questions are optional.

Privacy notice: This is a fictional class project. No real volunteer registration takes place. Use fictional information only. Do not enter real personal or medical details.

F4 · Requirements and Validation

Before beginning, please note:

  • Enter a fictional name and email address.
  • Choose an activity, day, and time.
  • Select your gardening experience level.
  • Enter between 1 and 20 available hours per week.
  • Optional comments are limited to 300 characters.
  • Complete the required acknowledgment.
